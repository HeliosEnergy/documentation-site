You add funds to a workspace wallet from the Wallet page, with a card payment. Amounts are in US dollars.

<Status state="live" />

## Before you start

- You need an Admin or Owner role in the organization. If you are a Member, ask an Admin or Owner to add the funds. See [Roles](/docs/accounts/roles).
- Select the workspace you want to fund in the workspace switcher. Each workspace has its own wallet, and the payment goes to the workspace that is selected.

## How do I add funds to my wallet?

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Wallet page

Sign in to the [console](https://console.helios.co), choose the workspace, and select **Wallet** in the sidebar.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose an amount

In **Add Funds**, select a **Quick Add** button, or type an amount in **Custom Amount** and select **Add Funds**. If the console does not accept the amount, it shows a message under the field.

</Step>
<Step>

### Complete the payment

The **Complete Payment** form shows the amount you are adding. Enter your card details in the payment form and select the **Pay** button, which shows the amount.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the balance

After the payment succeeds, the console shows a confirmation, and **Current Balance** and **Recent Transactions** update. If the balance does not change, refresh the page.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What if the payment fails?

If the card is declined, the form shows the error message and you stay on the payment form. Check the card details, try another card, or contact your card issuer. Select **Cancel** to go back to the Wallet page without paying.

If your card was charged and the balance has not changed after you refresh the Wallet page, contact the Helios team through the [Support page](/docs/support). Include the date, time and amount of the payment. Do not send card numbers.
