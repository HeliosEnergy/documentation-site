## Managing Billing

Follow these steps to effectively manage your Helios billing and payments.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    #### 1. Track Your Usage
    Navigate to the **Billing > Usage** tab to view detailed, real-time breakdowns of your GPU usage and associated costs.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    #### 2. Manage Payments
    Access the **Billing > Invoices** section to download past invoices and update your payment methods.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    #### 3. Set Budget Alerts
    Configure budget alerts to receive notifications when your spending approaches or exceeds predefined thresholds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Banner title="Billing Best Practices">
  - Set up budget alerts *before* running production-scale jobs.
  - Always download invoices for your records after payment confirmation.
  - Regularly review your usage to identify cost-saving opportunities.
</Banner>

<Banner preset="warning" title="Payment Failed?">
  If your payment fails, check these three things first:
  1.  Is your card information correct and up-to-date?
  2.  Has your card expired?
  3.  Does your card have sufficient funds?
  If you still have issues, please contact your bank or our support team.
</Banner>

## Billing FAQ

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Why did my payment fail?">
    The most common reason for payment failure is an incorrect credit card number or a card that has expired. Please double-check your payment information in the **Billing > Invoices** section. If the problem persists, contact your bank.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get a refund?">
    Please contact our support team with your invoice number and the reason for the refund request.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get a yearly plan?">
    Yes, we offer annual contracts with significant discounts for committed usage. Please contact our sales team to learn more.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
