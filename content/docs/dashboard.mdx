## Dashboard Overview

The sidebar gives you immediate access to all key resources:

- **Workspaces**: Organize your projects and teams.
- **Deployments**: Manage your running models and containers.
- **Billing**: Track usage and manage payments.
- **Support**: Get help from our team.
