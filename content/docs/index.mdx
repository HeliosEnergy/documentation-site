## Need a reserved GPU cluster?

Reserved GPU clusters are contracted capacity arranged with the Helios team rather than requested in the console. Read about [reserved clusters](/docs/bare-metal/reserved-clusters), see [pricing](https://www.helios.co/pricing), or [talk to our team](https://www.helios.co/contact). Helios also offers high-density [colocation](https://www.helios.co/colocation), which is covered on helios.co and not in these docs.
