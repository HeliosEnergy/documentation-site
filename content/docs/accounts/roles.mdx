---
title: Roles
description: What the Owner, Admin and Member roles can do in the Helios console, and how roles apply to organizations and workspaces.
---

Helios has three roles: Owner, Admin and Member. Owner is the highest role and Member is the lowest. Each role can do different things, as the table below shows.

## How do roles work?

- **Owner.** The person who signs up for an organization is its Owner. Owner is assigned when the organization is created and cannot be assigned or changed on the Users & Access page.
- **Admin.** Admins manage people and funding, and can allocate notebook nodes when allocation is available, as the table below shows. An Admin can give someone the Admin or Member role.
- **Member.** Members use the products in a workspace. Members cannot manage people or funding.

You hold a role in the organization and, separately, a role in each workspace you can access. The **Users & Access** page shows both. In the table below, Admin means Admin or Owner at the relevant level, and Owner means Owner.

You can manage only people whose role is lower than yours. Nobody can change a person with an equal or higher role. There is no Viewer role and no custom roles.

A guest, who is invited into a workspace from a different organization, holds only a workspace role. See [Users and access](/docs/accounts/users-and-access).

## What can each role do?

| Action | Member | Admin | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Use AI inference, the playground and request history | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Request bare metal access and manage your own SSH keys | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Start bare metal access | No | Yes | Yes |
| View Jupyter notebook nodes and their status | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Allocate, refresh and deallocate Jupyter notebook nodes | No | Yes | Yes |
| View the wallet balance and transactions | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Add funds and change wallet settings | No | Yes | Yes |
| View members and invitations | No | Yes | Yes |
| Invite people, and resend or revoke invitations | No | Yes | Yes |
| Change roles and remove people with a lower role | No | Yes | Yes |

Creating a workspace requires the Owner role.

Deleting a workspace and transferring ownership are not available in the console.

## How do I change a role?

Follow [How do I change someone's role?](/docs/accounts/users-and-access#how-do-i-change-someones-role) on the Users and access page. If you need an action that your role does not allow, ask an Admin or Owner in your organization.
