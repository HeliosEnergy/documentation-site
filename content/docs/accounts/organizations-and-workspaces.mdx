---
title: Organizations and workspaces
description: How Helios organizes accounts into organizations and workspaces, and why each workspace has its own wallet.
---

A Helios account has three levels. An organization owns the people and workspaces, a workspace is where you and your team use products, and each workspace has one wallet that pays for usage.

## How are accounts structured?

| Level | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Organization** | The ownership and membership boundary. Everyone who belongs to your account is a member of the organization. |
| **Workspace** | The team and GPU-access boundary. You use products inside a workspace. |
| **Wallet** | The prepaid balance for one workspace. Billing belongs to the workspace. |

One organization can have several workspaces. Each workspace has exactly one wallet.

## What does sign-up create?

When you sign up, Helios creates your organization, your Owner account, your first workspace and the wallet for that workspace. If you choose an **Individual** account, the organization is a personal account for one person. See [Create an account](/docs/getting-started/create-account).

## How do I switch between workspaces?

Use the workspace switcher at the top of the console sidebar. It shows your current workspace and the organization that owns it. Select another workspace in the list to switch. The products, members and wallet you see belong to the selected workspace.

## How do I create a workspace?

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Select **Create workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Enter a name in the **Create New Workspace** dialog and select **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Creating a workspace requires the Owner role. See [Roles](/docs/accounts/roles).

## Why use more than one workspace?

Use separate workspaces to keep teams, clients or projects apart. Because each workspace has its own wallet, usage in one workspace is billed separately from usage in the others, and you add funds to each wallet on its own. You choose who has access to each workspace on the [Users & Access](/docs/accounts/users-and-access) page.

## Can people from another organization work in my workspace?

Yes. If you invite an email address that already belongs to an account in a different Helios organization, that person joins your workspace as a guest and does not become a member of your organization. A guest sees your workspace in their workspace switcher, with your organization's name under it, and works with the products in that workspace. A guest does not see Users & Access. Usage in a workspace draws on that workspace's wallet, which belongs to the organization that owns it. See [Users and access](/docs/accounts/users-and-access).

## What can I not do?

The console does not currently offer a way to delete a workspace or to transfer ownership of an organization. To ask about either, use the [Support](/docs/support) page.
