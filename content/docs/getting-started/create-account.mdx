---
title: Create an account
description: Sign up for Helios with email and password, Google or Microsoft, and complete the email, phone and card steps.
---

You create a Helios account on the console sign-up page. You can register with an email address and password, with Google, or with Microsoft. Sign-up creates your organization, your first workspace and the wallet for that workspace, and you become the Owner.

<Callout type="info">
  Sign-up for email and password accounts includes email verification and phone number verification. Organization accounts that register with email and password also complete a card step. The console states that the card step does not charge you.
</Callout>

## How do I sign up?

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open the sign-up page

    Go to [console.helios.co](https://console.helios.co/login?tab=signup) and select the **Register** tab. If you are returning, use the **Login** tab instead.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose an account type

    The console asks you to choose **Individual** or **Organization**.

    - **Individual** creates a personal account. You enter an account name and a workspace name.
    - **Organization** is for a company or team. You enter an organization name, a short organization description, your role in the organization and a workspace name. If you register an organization with email and password, use a work email address. Addresses from personal mail providers such as Gmail are not accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Register with email or a provider

    Choose one of these.

    - **Email and password.** Enter your name, email address and password, then select **Create account**. Individual accounts also enter a phone number on this form.
    - **Google** or **Microsoft.** Select the provider and sign in with it. The console then asks for your account or organization name, a workspace name and a phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Complete verification

    The console guides you through the verification steps below. The order can differ by account type.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What verification is required?

### Phone number

Sign-up includes phone number verification. The console sends a 6-digit one-time code to the number you enter, and you type it into the **Verify Phone Number** screen. A phone number can belong to only one account.

### Card step for organization accounts

When you register an organization with email and password, the console shows a **Payment Verification** screen with the heading "Card Verification - No Charges". It states that the step does not charge your card. Enter the cardholder name, card details and billing address in the payment form, then select **Complete Verification**.

### Email address

If you register with email and password, you receive a verification link at your email address. Open the link to activate your account. If you do not see the message, check your spam folder or select **Resend verification email**. After you verify, return to the sign-in page.

## What happens after sign-up?

After you sign in, the console opens on the **Overview** page. Your organization, first workspace and wallet already exist. Next:

1. Take the [console tour](/docs/getting-started/console-tour).
2. [Add funds](/docs/billing/add-funds) to your wallet.
3. [Invite your team](/docs/accounts/users-and-access) if you work with others.

## What if I forget my password?

On the **Login** tab, select **Forgot password?**, enter the email address for your account and follow the link in the message. If an account exists for that address, Helios sends a reset link.

## What if sign-up does not work?

See [Troubleshooting](/docs/support/troubleshooting) for sign-in and verification problems, or read [Support](/docs/support) for how to reach Helios.
