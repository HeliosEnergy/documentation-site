To start using Helios, create an account at console.helios.co, choose a product and add funds to your workspace wallet. Each step below links to the full guide.

## How do I get started?

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Create an account

    Open [the sign-up page](https://console.helios.co/login?tab=signup) and register with email and password, Google or Microsoft. Sign-up creates your organization, your first workspace and the wallet for that workspace. Read [Create an account](/docs/getting-started/create-account) for the verification steps.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pick a product

    Choose the product that matches your workload.

    - [Bare metal](/docs/bare-metal) for a full node with 8 NVIDIA B300 GPUs and SSH access. Bare metal usage is recorded by the hour, pro-rated to elapsed time, starting at **Start access**.
    - [AI inference](/docs/inference) for models you call from the playground or the API.

    [Choose a product](/docs/getting-started/choose-a-product) compares these with Jupyter notebooks and reserved GPU clusters, which are not requested through the console.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add funds to your wallet

    AI inference needs funds in your workspace wallet, which is prepaid. Each workspace has its own wallet. Open **Wallet** in the console sidebar and use **Add Funds** to pay by card. Adding funds requires the Admin or Owner role, and the person who signs up is the Owner. See [Add funds](/docs/billing/add-funds).
  </Step>
</Steps>
