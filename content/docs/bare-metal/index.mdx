Bare metal gives you SSH access to a whole GPU node, not a share of one. In the Helios console you request a B300 node, Helios installs your SSH public key, and you connect over SSH.

<Status state="live" />

## What you get

- One or more nodes, each with 8 NVIDIA B300 GPUs (HGX B300).
- Dedicated SSH access using a key pair that you create. The private key stays on your machine.
- A live monitoring pane for GPU and host telemetry while your access is active.
- A time-limited access window with the accrued cost shown in the console.

## How it works

1. You open **Baremetal** in the console and select **B300**.
2. You choose how many nodes you need and paste an SSH public key. See [Request bare metal access](/docs/bare-metal/request-access).
3. Helios reviews the request. Approval is required for every request.
4. Helios installs your key and checks that SSH works.
5. You click **Start access**. The console shows the host, user, port and a ready-to-copy `ssh` command. See [Connect with SSH](/docs/bare-metal/connect-with-ssh).
6. Your access window begins when you start access and ends when the window closes. Bare metal usage is recorded by the hour, pro-rated to elapsed time, starting at **Start access**. See [Billing and access windows](/docs/bare-metal/billing-and-access-windows).

## What can you request in the console?

| Product | In the console | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| B300 (8 GPUs per node) | Request access | Follow [Request bare metal access](/docs/bare-metal/request-access). |
| RTX PRO 6000 bare metal | Contact us | Not yet available in the console. [Talk to the Helios team](https://www.helios.co/contact). |
| GB300 NVL72 | Contact us | Not yet available in the console. [Talk to the Helios team](https://www.helios.co/contact). |

For committed, reserved capacity see [Reserved clusters](/docs/bare-metal/reserved-clusters).

<Callout type="info" title="Approval comes first">
Submitting a request does not reserve a node, start the access window or show any node details. The access window and usage recording begin only after your SSH access is activated and you start it.
</Callout>
