This page explains how to contact the Helios team about the console and your Helios services, and what to include in your message.

## How to reach us

Use the [Helios contact page](https://www.helios.co/contact) form. Under **What are you planning?**, choose **Other**. Say in your message that it is a support matter, name the service, and describe the problem.

If you already work with a Helios contact, you can also message them directly.

## What should I include?

Include:

- Your organization and workspace names, and the email address you sign in with.
- The service affected: bare metal, Jupyter notebooks, AI inference, the API, or the wallet.
- The resource, if there is one: the node or access name, the notebook, the model name, or the request ID for an inference request.
- What you did, what you saw, what you expected, and when it started. Include any error message and its HTTP status.
- What you already tried. Check [Troubleshooting](/docs/support/troubleshooting) first.

## What should I not send?

- Private keys, passwords, API keys, tokens or other secrets.
- Card numbers.
- Personal data that the team does not need to see.

Share public SSH keys and key fingerprints. They are safe to share.

## Security concerns

If you think there has been unauthorized access to your account, an exposed credential, or a data exposure, act first and then tell us.

1. Revoke any API key that may be exposed, and create a replacement. See [Security](/docs/security).
2. Remove any SSH key you do not recognize from your bare metal access.
3. Use the [Helios contact page](https://www.helios.co/contact) form, choose **Other**, and say in your message that it is a security matter.

Describe what you saw and when. Do not include the secret itself.
